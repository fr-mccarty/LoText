LoText is a plain-text editor built around a simple idea: you work in one folder, and one file is open at a time.

There are no tabs, no preview pane, no split view. You open a folder, the files appear in the list, and you write. The text is plain text that any other app can read.

FOLDER BASED
• Work in LoText's own folder, or choose any folder the system picker can reach on your device.
• Create, rename, duplicate, move and delete files and folders right in the file list.
• Find File jumps to any file by name; Recently Edited brings back what you just worked on.
• Recent folders reopen with a tap.

MADE FOR TOUCH
• A bar above the keyboard with Tab, Shift-Tab, task boxes, bold, italic, undo and redo.
• Tasks, collapsible sections and indented lists, all in plain text.
• Long-press any file or folder for its menu.
• Side by side on a tablet; one pane at a time on a phone.

PRINTING THAT LOOKS FINISHED
• Markdown headings, paragraphs, tables and emphasis print as a properly set page.
• Print presets hold your margins, letterhead, headers, footers and page numbers.
• Print to any printer Android knows, or save the page as a PDF.

SAFE WITH YOUR TEXT
• Saves when you leave a file or the app, and checks first that nothing else changed it.
• Unsaved changes are kept safe if the app closes unexpectedly.

MORE THAN TEXT
• LoText also shows images, plays audio and video, and shows the text of documents (.docx, .odt, .rtf, .xlsx, .pptx) without opening them in the editor.

No account, no subscription, no ads, no tracking. Just your text.
